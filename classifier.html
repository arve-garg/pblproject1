Classifier evaluation

Train a Gaussian Naive Bayes classifier on a labelled dataset and inspect its holdout scores.

1. Upload labelled data

Use CSV or Excel with a target column containing class labels, such as Species, Outcome, or Category. Keep enough examples in every class.

No dataset uploaded yet.

2. Train and evaluate

Uses numeric feature columns, a deterministic stratified 80/20 holdout split, and Gaussian Naive Bayes. The test set is not used for training.

Evaluation metrics are dataset-specific; do not treat them as the accuracy of Lumen's rule-based agents.
